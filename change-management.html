Common questions

Change Management questions

My team hates change. Will this work?

People resist change that is done to them. When they help design it and can see their own ideas in it, most become its biggest supporters.

How do you measure adoption?

We agree measures before go-live, such as how many people use the new process and whether the old spreadsheet is still open, and check them after.

Do you only work on your own projects?

No. If you have already bought a system that isn't being used, we can help get it adopted.

Is this coaching or consulting?

Both. We help you plan and run the change, and coach the leaders who have to carry it.
