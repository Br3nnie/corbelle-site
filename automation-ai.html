Common questions

Automation & AI Quick Wins questions

Do we need to buy new software?

Usually not. We start with what you already pay for and only recommend something new when it clearly pays for itself.

Is it safe to use AI with our data?

It can be, with the right settings and rules. We set a simple standard for what can and can't go into AI tools before anyone starts.

What happens if an automation breaks?

Every automation has a named owner and plain-English notes, so your team knows how to fix or switch it off.

Can you build custom tools?

Yes. For bigger builds we work with a trusted engineering partner, scoped and checked by us.
