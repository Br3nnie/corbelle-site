Common questions

Joined-Up Systems questions

Do we have to replace our systems?

Rarely. Most businesses already own systems that can talk to each other. We connect what you have before suggesting anything new.

What about GDPR and data security?

We agree what data is shared, where it lives and who can see it, and bring in a security partner where the risk needs it.

Who builds the integrations?

We set up simple connections ourselves. Heavier engineering goes to a trusted data engineering partner, scoped and checked by us.

We bought a CRM and nobody uses it. Can you help?

Yes. That's usually a set-up and adoption problem, not a software one. We fix the set-up and switch off the side spreadsheets.
