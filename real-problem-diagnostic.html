The framework

CLEAR: five steps from complaint to confident action.

The same sequence is used every time, making the work consistent, teachable and repeatable.

C

Capture the complaint

Preserve the problem in the client’s own language before correcting it.

L

Link the consequence

Connect the issue to revenue, margin, capacity, cash, risk or customer outcomes.

E

Examine upstream

Trace purpose, process, people, information, tools and environment.

A

Assess the evidence

Separate observed facts from interpretations, assumptions and proposed solutions.

R

Recommend a response

Choose the smallest useful intervention before adding complexity.

The diagnostic test

Every problem must become testable.

When [specific condition] occurs, [group or process] cannot [required outcome], causing [measurable consequence]. We believe this is because [hypothesis]. Before committing to [investment], we will test [critical assumption] through [small intervention].
How it is delivered

Two focused weeks. One commercially important problem.

The work stays close to the people doing the job and ends with a decision—not a report that sits on a shelf.

Intake and scope

A short questionnaire, existing reports and one agreed problem. We define the decision, boundaries and stakeholders.

Investigation

Sponsor interview, three to six operator interviews, process observation and review of systems, workarounds and measures.

Diagnostic session

A 90-minute working session to map the system, challenge the assumed cause and locate the earliest controllable constraint.

Recommendation

A decision meeting covering what is happening, what remains uncertain, what to test first and what not to buy yet.

Optional intervention

A separate sprint to run the experiment, measure the result and embed the change without compromising the diagnosis.
