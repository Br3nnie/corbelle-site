How this started

The pressure to “do something with AI” is real. The starting point usually isn’t.

Every week, another executive is asked the same question: What are we doing with AI?

It might come from the board, an investor, a competitor announcement or a vendor promising that their tool will transform the business. The pressure is to show movement. So a licence gets bought, a pilot gets announced, and a team gets briefed before anyone has agreed what problem is actually being solved.

“Activity looks like progress right up until the tool launches—and nobody uses it.”

I built the Use Case Gameplan to put the thinking back in front of the spend. It takes one idea and asks the questions that are usually skipped: Is the problem real? Is the process ready? Is the data usable? Does anyone own the change? Will you know whether it worked?
