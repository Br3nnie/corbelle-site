Partnership

How we work with partners

A partner relationship with Corbelle follows one of two models. In both, the client gets one joined-up plan and nobody falls between the gaps.

Corbelle leads, specialists deliver

We own the client relationship, the diagnosis and the plan. When a workstream needs specialist skills, we bring in the right partner, brief them properly and hold the work to the same standard as our own.

Referral and joint work

You lead the client and spot a process or efficiency problem you don't cover. Introduce us, or bring us in alongside you as the optimisation workstream. Work flows both ways: we refer to you where it fits.

Principles

How we work, in practice

These come from twenty years of seeing multi-supplier projects go well, and badly.

Clear ownership

Everyone knows who owns the client, the scope, the decisions and the delivery.

Direct access to the work

Specialists get time with the people who do the work, not a second-hand brief.

Simple communication

Fewer handovers, fewer misunderstandings, faster progress.

One standard

Partner work is checked against the same plan and outcomes as ours before it reaches you.

Delivery approach

Every engagement starts with Corbelle. We find where the hours go and agree the plan with you before any specialist is involved, so partners are brought in to solve a defined problem, not to go looking for one.

When a partner joins, they work to the agreed plan, governance and reporting rhythm. We stay accountable for the outcome.

Our position on transparency

You will always know who is doing the work. We don't pass partners off as Corbelle staff, and we don't hide them behind our name. You'll meet them, know what they're responsible for, and know what their part costs. Where a partner or vendor pays Corbelle a revenue share, we say so up front.

Clarity

Roles and responsibilities

Corbelle's role

Diagnosis, the time audit, the plan, process redesign, choosing and briefing partners, quality checks, adoption and the client relationship. We're accountable for the outcome.

The partner's role

Delivering their specialist workstream (security, engineering, design or build) to the agreed scope, standard and timeline, and flagging early when assumptions change.

The client's role

Giving us access to the people who do the work, checking requirements and assumptions, and giving timely feedback so decisions get made quickly.
