Added vertical

Performance brands and fitness specialists are a credible second niche.

Gym groups, sports-performance businesses, rehabilitation providers and scaling fitness brands often rely heavily on member data, digital journeys, payments, security and small operational teams—without employing a CTO or CDO.

Why it fits

Technology is fundamental, but rarely the core expertise.

Useful triggers include a new CRM or member platform, fragmented athlete or client data, multi-site growth, AI-generated programming, cyber exposure, app adoption, poor system integrations and excessive practitioner administration.

Best targets: multi-site gym operators, boutique studio groups, performance and recovery brands, sports technology businesses, private rehabilitation providers and specialist training organisations.

Event operating system

Research first. Pre-book. Qualify. Follow up fast.

Attending is not the activity that creates pipeline. The work around the event does.

01

Three weeks before

Build 20 target accounts from exhibitors, speakers and attendees. Ask Thrive to flag existing ownership.

02

Pre-book

Invite MDs, COOs, CFOs and operations leaders to 15-minute meetings around a visible business trigger.

03

Qualify

Capture the complaint, consequence, assumed cause, proposed investment, owner and decision date.

04

Within 24 hours

Reflect the prospect’s problem in their own language and propose the paid Problem Clarity Session.
