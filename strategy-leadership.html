Common questions

Strategy & leadership questions

What is a fractional technology and operations lead?

A senior leader who works with you part-time. They set direction, manage suppliers and hold delivery to account, without the cost or commitment of a full-time hire.

What is an independent assessment?

An independent review of your technology, processes and risks. You get a written view of where you are, where you need to be and what to do first, written so a board or owner can act on it.

Can you help us choose a supplier or IT partner?

Yes. We set clear requirements, run a fair head-to-head between suppliers and give you one recommendation you can stand behind. Some vendors pay us a revenue share. Where they do, we tell you before you choose, and the recommendation is still based on what fits you.

What's the difference between a strategy and a roadmap?

A strategy decides what matters and why: people, process, technology and risk. A roadmap is the sequenced plan that follows from it, with owners and dates. We can deliver both, together or in sequence.
