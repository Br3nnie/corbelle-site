What we offer

Everything you need to decide what to fix first.

Time mapping

We interview and shadow the people doing the work to see how a normal week really runs, not how the process document says it does.

↳ A true picture of where the hours go

Process mapping

Every manual, repeated step drawn out end to end, including the workarounds nobody wrote down.

↳ One shared view of how work flows

Cost of busywork

We put a £ figure on every leak, using your own salaries and volumes, so priorities are based on money, not opinion.

↳ A cost on every manual step

Ranked fix list

Fixes ranked by value and effort: what to simplify, what to automate, what to join up and what to leave alone.

↳ Biggest wins first, quick wins flagged

Clear-out by exception

Reports, spreadsheets and steps nobody uses get retired before anything is automated or migrated.

↳ Less to maintain from day one

Playback session

We play findings back in your team's own words, grouped into themes, so everyone can see their view in the decision.

↳ A team that backs the plan

Common questions

Time & Process Audit questions

How long does an audit take?

It depends on how many teams are in scope. We agree the timeline and the price before we start.

Will it disrupt my team?

Very little. Most people spend under an hour with us, and we watch the work as it happens rather than pulling people into workshops.

Do you need access to our systems?

Only to look, not to change anything. Seeing the real spreadsheets and screens is how we find the workarounds.

What happens after the audit?

You own the findings. Many clients ask us to fix the top items, but you're free to do it yourselves or with someone else.
