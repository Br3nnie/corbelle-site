Cookie policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

This website doesn't set any cookies of its own, and we don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. That's why you won't see a cookie banner.

What does happen

Fonts. Our typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google's servers to download them, which shares your IP address with Google. Google Fonts doesn't set cookies.

Hosting. Our hosting provider, Vercel, may use strictly necessary technical measures to deliver the site securely. These don't track you.

Third-party sites. When you click through to book a call (Cal.com), subscribe to the newsletter (Kit), visit LinkedIn or open one of our separately hosted tools, you're on their site and their cookie policies apply.

If this changes

If we ever add analytics or other non-essential cookies, we'll ask for your consent first and update this page.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Blocking cookies won't affect how this site works.

Questions: hello@corbelle.ai. See also our privacy policy.
