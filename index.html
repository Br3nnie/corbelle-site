FAQ

Common questions.

What actually happens on the free time audit?

A 30-minute call. You talk me through how a typical week runs. I'll point out where the hours are likely leaking and whether we're a fit. No pitch deck.

Do we need to buy new software?

Usually not. Most of the wins come from simplifying the process and using tools you already pay for — Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Xero and the like.

What size of business do you work with?

Owner-led UK businesses with roughly 50 to 400 staff. Big enough for the waste to hurt, small enough to change fast.

How long does a project take?

The Find stage takes about a week. Most Fix projects deliver in 4–8 weeks.

How is pricing structured?

Fixed price, agreed up front, tied to a defined scope. No day rates, no surprises.

Will my team resist it?

Not if they're involved from day one. We design the changes with the people who do the work — that's the whole point.
