The six decisions

The six decisions behind every successful AI rollout

Six stages. Each answers one decision, then hands off cleanly to the next. Without a connected method, every new idea creates another debate, another spreadsheet, another set of assumptions.

1

Readiness Diagnostic

Find where leadership conviction, ownership, evidence, data and delivery capacity don't yet line up.

  • Assess readiness across the areas that matter
  • Surface disagreements before they become delivery problems
  • Identify missing owners, evidence and capabilities
  • Establish the conditions required to proceed
The decision: Are we ready to do this properly?
2

Use Case Identification & Prioritisation

Turn a crowded list of possibilities into a ranked, defensible portfolio.

  • Capture and compare potential use cases consistently
  • Separate visible excitement from genuine business value
  • Evaluate feasibility, strategic fit, risk and urgency
  • Decide what moves now, later or not at all
The decision: What should go first?
3

AI Audit

Understand the current process, establish the baseline, and test whether the economics are credible.

  • Map how the work happens today
  • Identify friction, waste and avoidable cost
  • Define measurable outcomes and success thresholds
  • Build an evidence-based case to stop, fix, test or scale
The decision: Will it create enough value?
4

Tool & Solution Match

Define what the organisation needs before committing to a platform, vendor or build.

  • Translate the use case into clear requirements
  • Compare build, buy and configure options
  • Evaluate fit, integration, data and exit risk
  • Make a decision that can be explained and defended
The decision: What should we use?
5

Governance & Policy

Put proportionate rules, data boundaries, approvals and accountability around AI use.

  • Set clear expectations for acceptable use
  • Classify risk and data sensitivity
  • Define approval routes and decision ownership
  • Make safe use easier without stopping useful experimentation
The decision: Can we do this safely and responsibly?
6

Rollout & Adoption

Turn implementation into changed behaviour and measurable use.

  • Plan the rollout around real users and workflows
  • Build a champions and stakeholder approach
  • Create practical training and support
  • Track adoption, feedback and leading indicators
The decision: Will people use it, and will the value appear?

Where it goes wrong

The mistakes this system is built to catch

I built the Suite around a pattern I see repeatedly. The most expensive AI mistakes rarely happen because someone can't use the technology. They happen in the gap between the idea and the implementation:

  • The problem was never defined precisely
  • The baseline was missing
  • Ownership was assumed, not assigned
  • The tool came before the requirements
  • Governance arrived too late
  • Adoption was reduced to training and launch comms

The Suite makes those decisions visible and gives you a practical way to work through them.
