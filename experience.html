Experience that speaks for itself

Twenty years inside nine organisations, from Big 4 audit to Head of Data, and now Corbelle clients from multi-country groups to trade businesses. Here's the evidence.

Proven in practice

Selected examples. Client names are withheld unless they've said yes.

International corporate finance group

Financial services · offices across Europe

The situation

The group had grown country by country, each with its own IT and licences. When a senior executive asked who could see and change what, nobody could answer it, and the board wanted comfort fast.

What we delivered

An independent assessment, a fair selection across three IT partners, and a governance model: one minimum security standard, with each country free to choose its own tools, AI included, inside it.

Admin roles going from ~28 to 4 · one IT partner chosen · Phase 3 on the table

AssessmentPartner selectionGovernance

Fencing & landscaping firm

Trade business · South East England

The situation

Great on site, buried in admin. Every survey meant going back later to work out a price, and customers were left chasing for a week. Off-the-shelf apps had been tried before and didn't stick.

What we delivered

A phone app built around how the team already works: say the job out loud and get a priced PDF quote using the firm's own rates, sent by WhatsApp while still on site.

~3 seconds to price a spoken job · no forms, no evening admin
