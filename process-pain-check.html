    Questions leaders usually ask
    Useful before clever.
    Is this an AI assessment?
    No. It begins with work that is painful or expensive. AI may be one possible response, but the result can also point to simplification, better ownership, integration, training or stopping unnecessary work.
    Is the cost figure a saving guarantee?
    No. It is the loaded value of time currently consumed, shown as a conservative 25–50% recoverable range. Releasing capacity is not automatically a cash saving; the value appears only when the time is removed, redeployed or used to serve more demand.
    What if I already have an AI use case?
    Use the £7 Use Case Gameplan to pressure-test it. This check is designed for the earlier moment when the problem is felt but the right intervention is not yet defined.
    Can I use it for more than one process?
    Yes. Run it once per process. Comparing separate results is more useful than blending several workflows into one answer.
    Who is it for?
    Operations, finance, technology, transformation and functional leaders in growing organisations—especially where manual work, workarounds, delays or repeated errors are absorbing meaningful capacity.
