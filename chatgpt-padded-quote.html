Buying

Before you approve a ChatGPT-padded tech quote

What to check when an AI-written proposal suddenly costs a quarter more.

Brendan EllisNov 2026 · 2 min read

A business owner told me recently about a technology proposal they'd been sent.

Before signing, they ran it through ChatGPT to check it.

ChatGPT suggested extra features, extra security, extra integrations.

The price went up by a quarter.

Not one of those additions was needed.

AI is very good at "more"

Ask an AI tool to review a proposal and it will rarely say "this is too much". It will tell you what's missing. It will think of edge cases. It will make everything sound important.

That's useful for spotting gaps. It's dangerous for deciding scope.

Because the AI doesn't know your business. It doesn't know your budget, your team, or that you have 60 staff, not 6,000.

And suppliers use it too

Proposals are now easier to write than ever. Longer documents, more options, more polish.

A long, polished proposal looks thorough. It isn't always right.

Five checks before you sign

1. What problem does each line solve? If nobody can name the problem in one sentence, it's a nice-to-have.

2. What do we already own? Most businesses pay for software that already does half of what's being proposed.

3. What happens if we leave it out? If the answer is "nothing much, for a year", leave it out for a year.

4. Who will actually use it? Features nobody uses still cost licences and maintenance.

5. What does it cost to run, not just to build? Support, licences, AI usage and the time your team spends looking after it.

Use AI, but keep the judgement

I'm not saying don't use AI to review proposals. Use it. It's a great second pair of eyes.

But treat its suggestions as questions, not answers.

The decision needs someone who understands your business, your people and your numbers. Ideally someone with no reason to make the project bigger.

If you'd like a second opinion on a proposal before you sign, that's exactly the kind of conversation a free time audit is for.
